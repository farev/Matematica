## Candidate slate (external)

**C1 — w(2;5,8), mixed van der Waerden numbers (Ramsey theory on the
integers).** Statement: least n such that every 2-coloring of [1,n] contains a
monochromatic 5-AP in color 0 or a monochromatic 8-AP in color 1. Sources
checked 2026-08-16 (all secondary, via snippets): Ahmed, "Some More Van der
Waerden Numbers", J. Integer Seq. 16 (2013) art. 13.4.4 — abstract reports
"the exact value of the previously unknown w(2;5,7)" = 260, with w(2;5,5)=178,
w(2;5,6)=206 the only prior values in the row; no snippet anywhere reports a
value or bound for w(2;5,8); Landman–Robertson's mixed-vdW line and
OEIS A217037 track the adjacent tables. Why believed open: the row's ledger
stops at 260 in every retrievable snippet, and the exact-value community
visibly moved to asymptotics after 2014 (Green, Forum Math. Pi 2022 — the
w(3,k) super-polynomial lower bound) and to certificates for Ramsey numbers
(the 2025 verified-certificates paper for R(3,8)/R(3,9), arXiv 2502.06055).
Caveat recorded: a BOINC project (boincsynergy.ca; Monroe's vdwnumbers line,
arXiv 1603.03301) grinds van der Waerden *lower bounds* by Rabung-style
colorings; its pages were unreadable today, so whether it has touched this
cell is unknown. Risk accepted and stated.

**C2 — open cells of Arora–Dwivedi–Tripathi, "Improved Two-Colour Rado
Numbers for Linear Equations with Certain Coefficients", EJC 32(4) #P4.63
(Dec 2025) (secondary).** The paper determines exact 2-color Rado numbers for
Σaᵢxᵢ = xₘ with 2-/3-distributable coefficient sets "and bounds otherwise" —
those bound-only cells are the freshest possible open-cells target, and the
instances are small (Rado scale, well inside this box). Not chosen **because
the paper is unreachable**: the list of open cells, and even the definition of
t-distributability, could not be pinned through snippets. Attacking "their
open cells" without being able to state which cells those are fails the
honesty bar. Revisit from a session with literature access.

**C3 — tightness of ART(5), the abelian repetition threshold over 5 letters
(combinatorics on words).** Samsonov–Shur conjectured their lower bounds on
ART(k) tight; Petrova–Shur ("Abelian Repetition Threshold Revisited",
arXiv 2109.09306) refuted tightness for k = 6..10 and left k = 5 as the case
still conjectured tight (secondary). Deciding it means proving avoidance at
the threshold — Dekking-style template machinery for abelian powers, an idea
bottleneck held by an active school, not a compute bottleneck. Scored out.

Subfields spanned: Ramsey theory on the integers, partition regularity of
linear equations, combinatorics on words.

Also examined and rejected during the survey: **w(2;3,20)** (conjectured 389
by Ahmed–Kullmann–Snevily, DAM 2014) — the natural headline target, killed by
feasibility: the t=19 predecessor consumed ~196 CPU-years on 200 Opterons in
2011 (secondary, snippet of the C&C literature); no 4-core session touches
t=20 honestly. **Erdős-problems database mining** — the 2026 AI wave (Quanta,
2026-08-03, secondary) is actively strip-mining precisely the
computationally-flavored entries; scooped-risk too high to verify through
snippets alone. **Weak Schur WS(6) ≥ 646** (Ageron et al. 2021, secondary) —
record-chasing against specialist local-search pipelines with 4 cores is a
coin flip with no certificate to show for a miss.

## Selection

**C1, w(2;5,8).** (a) The bottleneck is finite search with a known encoding —
two clause families over n Boolean variables — on hardware and solvers twelve
years past the frontier's last movement; the certification toolkit
(`tools/satcert`, built 08-05, validated but never yet used by a shipped
result) closes the proof gap the 2013 computations left open. (b)
Already-done check: the row's ledger (Ahmed's JIS paper, the Landman tables,
OEIS) stops at 260 in every snippet retrieved today; residual risk is the
unreadable BOINC project, stated above. (c) The result extends the
Landman–Robertson/Ahmed–Kullmann exact-values line — the tables every
Ramsey-theory-on-integers survey carries — and lands in OEIS's mixed-vdW
sequences; the certificates extend the 2025 verified-certificates practice
(arXiv 2502.06055) from Ramsey numbers to van der Waerden numbers.

What counts as achieving it: `w(2;5,8)` decided — a verified witness at w−1
plus a RUP-checked UNSAT proof at w. Graceful degradation, in order: (i) the
certified ladder t ≤ 7 (first proof-backed derivations of the row) plus a
resumable cube-and-conquer campaign at the (5,8) boundary with a committed
per-cube certificate manifest; (ii) an improved certified lower bound
`w(2;5,8) > n` from a verified witness at the largest n reached. Mid-session
checkpoint: if the calibration ladder prices the (5,8) UNSAT beyond a
resumable multi-session campaign (extrapolated growth against the measured
(4,6)→(4,7)→(4,8) and (5,5)→(5,6)→(5,7) curves), re-aim the UNSAT campaign at
the cheapest open cell the extrapolation admits and say so here.
