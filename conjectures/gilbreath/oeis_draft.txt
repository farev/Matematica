OEIS submission draft (two sequences) — Gilbreath continuous-model constants
=============================================================================

Context. Chase, Hunter, Tao (arXiv:2607.08712, 2026), Section 1.3, define
c_i = E[a_{(i,j)}] for the Gilbreath array (iterated absolute differences)
of i.i.d. standard exponential random variables. They compute c_0..c_3 and
note the c_i are rational. The values for i = 4, 5, 6 below are new,
computed by exact sign-chamber cone decomposition with a partition-of-unity
certificate in Q (this repository, ck_exact_certified.py).

Name (A): Numerator of the expected value at depth n of the Gilbreath
  (iterated absolute difference) array generated by i.i.d. standard
  exponential random variables.
Name (B): Denominator of the same.

Comments:
  - c_n = A(n)/B(n); c_0 = c_1 = 1, c_2 = 7/9, c_3 = 227/288 are due to
    Chase-Hunter-Tao; c_4, c_5, c_6 computed 2026-07-28 by exact
    polyhedral-cone integration (2^(n(n+1)/2) sign chambers; certificate:
    chamber exponential measures sum to exactly 1 in Q).
  - Chase-Hunter-Tao prove Sum_{i<=n} c_i >= log(n+e) and
    c_n >= exp(-Sum_{i<n} c_i); a two-channel refinement gives
    c_n >= 2*exp(-Sum_{i<n} c_i) for n >= 2 (this repository, NOTE.md,
    Theorem 2).
  - B(n) is smooth (largest prime factors 1, 1, 3, 3, 17, 47, 331), being
    a product of cone ray-coordinate sums; A(n) contains large prime
    factors.

References:
  Z. Chase, Z. Hunter, T. Tao, "Gilbreath's conjecture: a Cramér random
  model and a deterministic analysis", arXiv:2607.08712 (2026).
